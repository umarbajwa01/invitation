A Beautiful Beginning — Piano & Warm Strings

Original 32-second instrumental composed and synthesized for this invitation.
Delicate additive piano and a warm sustained string-like pad, 60 BPM, stereo.
No vocals, speech, third-party recordings, samples, or quoted melodies.
Circular note tails and reverberation support seamless sample-accurate looping.
Playback gain is capped at 0.20, with a two-second fade-in.
